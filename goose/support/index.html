Support — GOOSE

Last updated: June 17, 2026

GOOSE is a social app-blocking app that lets groups go phone-free together during shared sessions. It is built and operated by AIRGATE LLC.

Need Help?

Email us at contact@airgateapp.com. We aim to respond within a few business days.

Report Abuse or a Safety Concern

You can report objectionable content or abusive users directly in the app: long-press a participant and choose Report, or open the session's ··· menu and choose Report Session. You can also block any user to hide them from your view. For urgent safety concerns, email contact@airgateapp.com and we will review it as appropriate.

Delete Your Account and Data

You can delete your account and associated data in the app: open Profile → Delete Account. You can also request deletion by emailing contact@airgateapp.com.
